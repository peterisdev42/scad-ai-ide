# Repair and verification

`[10:0:100]` has an invalid step. Quick Fix removes the invalid explicit step, retaining the range. It never chooses a new engineering threshold. Defaults outside a range or off-grid require an explicit user decision. Expression defaults are not evaluated or silently frozen.

Ordinary variables and prose do not establish control intent. Unsupported expressions with explicit hints are information-level candidates. Preserve comments, group scope, geometry and preset files. New inserted parameters do not automatically wire themselves into geometry.

Analysis can be incomplete because of syntax, oversized text, or bounded output. Treat complete=false as unverified. Warning filtering never turns a failed validation into a pass. CLI success does not prove the requested controls; source validation does not prove rendering or physical correctness.


Repeated assignments are not automatically invalid syntax. Analyze and validate the effective final declaration; older overridden hints/defaults do not determine the current widget. A missing control caused by a final expression is informational until an explicit target requires that control. Deliberate Hidden declarations need no duplicate warning. Structured edits may still refuse an ambiguous multi-declaration target; use reviewed ordinary edits instead of interpreting that refusal as a syntax error.
